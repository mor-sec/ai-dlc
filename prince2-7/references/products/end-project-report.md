# End project report

**Purpose:** used by the project board to evaluate the project and authorise closure, after the products have been accepted and handed over.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Created in** | Closing a project (CP) |
| **Prepared by** | Project manager |
| **Received by** | Project board, in DP (authorise project closure) |
| **Derived from** | PID, business case, project plan with actuals, project log, quality register, lessons log, acceptance records |

## Composition
1. **PM's report**: summary of performance
2. **Review of the business case**: benefits achieved so far, residual benefits expected after the project, deviations from the approved case, and how post-project benefits reviews will happen
3. **Review of objectives**: performance against the seven targets, including the project-level tolerances and how the project performed against its approaches
4. **Review of team performance**: recognition and capability lessons
5. **Review of products**:
   - quality records
   - approval and **acceptance** records, from users and from those who will operate and maintain the products
   - off-specifications and concessions
   - a handover summary
6. **Lessons**: reference the lessons report
7. **Follow-on action recommendations**: open issues and risks handed to operations or the business layer, each with an owner

## Quality criteria
- Acceptance is evidenced by the right roles, not assumed.
- Every open issue and risk is either closed or assigned for follow-on.
- Benefits not yet realised have a review date and an owner.
- The report covers premature closure honestly, if that applies.

**Ends with:** decision required. Authorise project closure, release resources, and send the closure notification. Also approve the benefits review plan.
