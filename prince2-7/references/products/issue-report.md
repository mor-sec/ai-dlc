# Issue report

**Purpose:** describes, assesses and recommends a resolution for an issue that is being managed formally: a request for change, an off-specification, or a problem or concern.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Created in** | CS: capture and examine issues and risks |
| **Prepared by** | Project manager (can be drafted by anyone, including an agent) |
| **Decided by** | The level in the issue's severity: team manager, PM, change authority, board or business layer |
| **Derived from** | Issue register entry, impact analysis against plans and business case |

## Composition
1. Issue identifier (matches the issue register) and issue type
2. Date raised and raised by
3. Issue report author and date
4. **Issue description**: what has happened or is requested, and its cause
5. **Impact analysis**: effect on the seven performance targets, on plans at each level, on the business case and on the risk profile
6. Recommendation
7. Priority and severity (who can decide)
8. **Decision**, decision by, and decision date (left blank until a human decides)
9. Closure date

## Quality criteria
- The issue is stated clearly and has been checked against the register for duplicates.
- Impact analysis covers all affected products (use the product register and product descriptions).
- The recommendation includes its cost and time effect and its source of funding (for example the change budget).
- The decision field is never filled in by Claude or an agent unless a human has confirmed the decision.

**AI notes:** agents commonly raise these when a work package hits a scope boundary, such as a needed change outside `scope_allow`. The agent drafts items 1–6. The human at the right severity level decides.
